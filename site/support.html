Support

Get help

Found a bug, or stuck on something? Open an issue on the Bow Season issue tracker. You need a free GitHub account to post. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what happened just before the problem.

Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.

How do I shoot?

Touch the lower half of the screen and drag down to draw the bow. The arrow flies the opposite way from your drag, and a longer drag shoots farther. The power meter shows how hard you are pulling.

A dotted arc and a small reticle show where the arrow will land, with the wind already counted. Let go to shoot. To cancel, slide your thumb back to where you started, then let go.

Don't hold full draw too long. After a couple of seconds your arms get tired and the reticle starts to sway. The arrow also takes a moment to land, so aim a little ahead of an animal that is walking.

Where are my harvests stored?

On your phone. The journal, your trophy mounts, and your settings live in the game's own storage. Nothing goes to a server, and we cannot see your harvests. If you delete the game, the journal goes with it. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I move my journal to a new phone?

No. Bow Season has no accounts and no cloud sync, so the game has no way to send your journal from one phone to another. Your journal stays on the phone where you earned it.
