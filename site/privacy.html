BOW SEASON

Privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Bow Season collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no purchases. Your journal stays on your phone. If you sign in to Game Center, two of your scores go to Apple's Game Center leaderboards.

Paper Street App LLC ("we") makes Bow Season, a bow-hunting game for iPhone. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short answer is nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Bow Season does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. We never see your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device ID, or how you play.

What stays on your phone

The game saves a few things so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Your journal: each harvest's species, weight, antler points, spot, season, and time.
  • The bucks you mounted in the trophy room.
  • Your last level, season, and hunting spot.

This data lives only in the game's storage on your phone. The game never sends it anywhere, except the two Game Center scores described below, and we have no copy of it. If you delete Bow Season, iOS deletes this data with it.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include the game's data in that backup. That backup is between you and Apple. We never see it.

Sharing a trophy

When you tap SHARE on a mount, the game draws a picture of it and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where the picture goes. The game does not send it anywhere on its own, and we never receive it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, Bow Season sends two numbers to Apple's Game Center: your best buck score and the total pounds of meat in your freezer. Game Center shows them on the BIGGEST BUCK and FREEZER (LB) leaderboards with your Game Center name, to the players your Game Center settings allow. Apple runs Game Center under Apple's privacy policy. We see those leaderboards the same way any player does, and we receive nothing else about you. If you are not signed in to Game Center, the game sends nothing. To stop sending scores, sign out of Game Center in Settings.

Game updates

When the game opens, it checks Expo's update service for fixes to the game. That check sends the game's version and the kind of device it runs on, and like any internet request it reveals your IP address to Expo. It sends nothing from your journal and nothing that identifies you. Expo handles these requests under Expo's privacy policy.

Permissions

Bow Season asks for no permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo library. It plays sounds and haptics, and it never records audio.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting code. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Apple handles your App Store download under Apple's privacy policy. If you turned on Share with App Developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us crash reports and usage totals that do not identify you.

Children

Bow Season collects no information from anyone, including children under 13. A child can play it without sharing anything with us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. The full history of this page is public in the Bow Season repository.

Questions

To ask about this policy, open an issue on the Bow Season issue tracker. See the support page for details.
